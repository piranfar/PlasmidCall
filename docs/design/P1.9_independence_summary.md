**Date:** 2026-08-17 · **Stage:** P1.9B part D

`P1.9_study_overlap_audit.csv` · `P1.9_unresolved_identity_table.csv` ·
`P1.9_proposed_exclusions.csv`

Four datasets that actually exist were compared. **No 385-genome cohort was included** — it
does not exist (`CATALOGUE_NOT_FOUND`).

| Dataset | Independent units | Identifier state |
|---|---|---|
| `DEV_250` — internal development benchmark | 250 | institutional IDs only, **0/250 accessions** |
| `PLASEVAL_53` — PlasEval *E. coli* | 53 | BioSample + SRA + GCA, complete |
| `PG2_436` — public-source plasgraph2 candidate | 436 | 426/436 BioSample, 422 short-read runs |
| `CG137` — located complete-genome collection | 137 | assembly + BioSample accessions |

---

## 1. The audit found a real overlap

**`SAMN02700075` — *Staphylococcus aureus* NRS100, `GCF_000626615.1`, BioProject `PRJNA231221`**
is present in **both** the plasgraph2 `eskapee-test` partition (as
`robertson-benchmark_saur-SAMN02700075`, source study Robertson2018) **and** the located
137-complete-genome collection.

One sample in 436 is a small number, but it is the whole point of the exercise: the corpora
are not disjoint, and a cohort assembled without this check would have carried a genuine
leak. It is proposed for exclusion from any analysis that also uses `CG137`.

Every comparison involving the development benchmark is `IDENTIFIER_INCOMPLETE`, **not**
independent. Its 250 identifiers are institutional strings in a namespace that cannot
intersect an archive namespace, so a null result carries no evidential weight. This is
recorded on all 436 rows as `vs_DEV250_any: IDENTIFIER_INCOMPLETE`.

`INDEPENDENT_BY_IDENTIFIER` is **provisional**: every row also carries
`sequence_relatedness: SEQUENCE_RELATEDNESS_PENDING`.

strata:** all 254 held-out samples have public short reads and archival identifiers.
