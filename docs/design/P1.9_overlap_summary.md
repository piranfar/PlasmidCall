**Date:** 2026-08-17 · **Stage:** P1.9A part D

## Headline

**No identifier collision was found between plasgraph2-datasets and any project dataset — and
not one sample is yet labelled independent.** Zero overlap at Layer 1 and Layer 2 is a
necessary condition for independence, not a sufficient one. Layer 4 (sequence relatedness)
has not run, and for the development benchmark Layers 1–2 are **not computable at all**.

Every one of the 436 samples therefore carries `INDEPENDENCE_NOT_YET_DETERMINED`.

## 3. Layer 1 and Layer 2 — exact and normalised identifiers

**Versus PlasEval (computable, and executed).** Accessions were normalised by trimming
version suffixes and upper-casing.

| Comparison | Result |
|---|---|
| BioSample ∩ BioSample | **NONE** |
| Short-read SRA ∩ short-read SRA | **NONE** |
| Long-read SRA ∩ long-read SRA | **NONE** |
| Any identifier ∩ any identifier (53 × 4 vs 436 × 3) | **NONE** |

**Versus the development benchmark (not computable).** Its 250 identifiers are institutional
strings; the candidate's are archive accessions. The two namespaces cannot intersect *by
construction*, so a null result here carries **no evidential weight whatsoever**. Recording
"no match" as evidence of independence would be exactly the error of treating a different
display name as a different isolate.

**Recovery path:** Supplementary Table 1 of doi:10.1093/bib/bbaf589 maps the internal IDs to
BioSample accessions. The article's data statement confirms this. Until that table is
obtained and joined, dev-benchmark independence stays `INDEPENDENCE_NOT_YET_DETERMINED`.

**Versus the ~385 complete genomes.** No catalogue was found in the Warehouse or in the
read-only related repositories. Owner input required.

## 5. Layer 4 — sequence relatedness (planned, not executed)

**No sequence data were downloaded.** The plan, to run only after an approved import gate:

| Step | Method | Threshold |
|---|---|---|
| Sketching | Mash or sourmash sketches of every assembly | k=21, s=10,000 (predeclare) |
| Genome clustering | ANI via fastANI/skani, single-linkage | predeclare, proposed 99.5% |
| Plasmid clustering | pairwise replicon alignment, cluster by identity × coverage | predeclare, proposed 95% identity over 80% of the shorter replicon |
| Exact-sequence check | SHA-256 of contig sequences and of normalised GFA segment sets | exact match |
| Cross-corpus test | candidate × {dev benchmark, PlasEval, 385 genomes} | any hit → `POSSIBLE_RELATEDNESS` or stronger |

A sample is promoted to `INDEPENDENT_CONFIRMED` only when it passes Layers 1–3 **and** Layer 4.
Thresholds must be frozen before the comparison is run.

## 8. Conclusions

1. **plasgraph2-datasets shows no identifier overlap with PlasEval.** That is a genuinely
   encouraging result for the independence case — but it is one layer of four.
2. **Independence from the development benchmark cannot presently be tested**, because that
   benchmark publishes no archival accessions. This is the single largest unresolved gap and
   has a concrete fix: Supplementary Table 1 of doi:10.1093/bib/bbaf589.
3. **120 samples carry a live study-overlap risk** through the MOB-suite corpus.
4. **Sequence-level deduplication remains mandatory** before any sample is called independent.
5. The *E. coli* stratum is small (18 samples). Cross-species strata are far larger (254
   samples, 39 species) and are where this corpus is most valuable.
