# P1.12 Phase 0 fit waiver

**Dated 2026-08-20.** Narrow, single-purpose authorization. Nothing here grants a broader training
permission, and the historical Phase 0 prohibition is not repealed.

---

## 1. The original Phase 0 restriction

`AGENTS.md` states, and continues to state:

> ## Current phase
>
> This repository is in **Phase 0: architecture, inventory, reconciliation, and controlled ingestion**.
>
> Do not train, fit, tune, or rerun Model 1 or Model 2 during Phase 0.

That rule governed the earlier phase and remains the default posture of this repository. It is
preserved verbatim, not deleted and not rewritten. This waiver sits beside it.

A precedent exists: `docs/plans/P1.10_PHASE0_FIT_WAIVER.md` granted the equivalent narrow permission
for the P1.10 work.

## 2. The authorization

The repository owner authorized, on **2026-08-20**, the fitting and freezing of a second
PlasmidCall estimator for the general (non-core-ARG) domain, designated **v1.2-General**, together
with the predeclaration of its prospective P1.11 evaluation.

The authorization was given in writing as a GO decision following a scoped 47-isolate feasibility
probe and a corrected diagnostic suite, and was reaffirmed with two contract corrections and one
governance correction on the same date.

## 5. Permitted estimator and feature schema

**Estimator.** Standardised logistic regression, and nothing else:

```
SimpleImputer(strategy="median", add_indicator=False)
StandardScaler()
LogisticRegression(max_iter=4000, C=1.0, solver="lbfgs", random_state=20260821)
```

**Feature schema.** The 12 frozen categorical tool calls only — 102 columns over a fixed
tool order and a fixed 7-term vocabulary, written as constants and not learned from data. No
continuous native features, no graph features, no taxonomic features.

**Missing-tool neutrality.** A tool in a state never observed during training has its 8-column
block imputed to the training means, so the post-scaling block is exactly zero and its logit
contribution is exactly zero. States observed in training keep their learned encoding.

**Tools not run.** PlasmidHunter and gplasCC were not run, by instruction and by redundancy.

## 6. Threshold freeze

The standard operating threshold is frozen at **0.9285**, derived to a development PPV target of
0.970.

## 7. No further fitting

After this freeze:

- v1.2-General must not be refitted, recalibrated or rethresholded.
- v1.1 must not be modified in any respect.
- No third threshold-selection round is permitted.
- No additional comparator may be introduced and refitted at P1.11 reporting time.

## 8. P1.11 execution remains separately gated

This waiver authorizes **fitting and freezing only**. It does not authorize:

- acquiring P1.11 reads
- assembling P1.11 isolates
- running inference on P1.11
- generating P1.11 truth
- starting any OCI compute

Each of those requires its own explicit authorization. The predeclared evaluation design is
recorded in [P1.11_PROSPECTIVE_EVALUATION_AMENDMENT.yaml](P1.11_PROSPECTIVE_EVALUATION_AMENDMENT.yaml)
and is sealed pending that separate decision.

## 9. Scope statement

**No broader training authorization is implied.** This waiver covers exactly one estimator, fitted
once, on one named dataset, with one frozen feature schema and one frozen threshold. Any other
fitting, tuning or retraining in this repository remains prohibited under the Phase 0 rule until
separately waived in writing.
