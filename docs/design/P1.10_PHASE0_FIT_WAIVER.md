# P1.10 — Recorded waiver: model fitting during Phase 0

**Date:** 2026-08-19 · **Status:** recorded, owner-authorised · **Scope:** narrow, see §3

## 1. The rule being waived

`AGENTS.md:15` — *"Do not train, fit, tune, or rerun Model 1 or Model 2 during Phase 0."*
`docs/PROJECT_GOVERNANCE.md:9` — *"Do not train or rerun scientific models during Phase 0."*

## 2. Why a waiver is needed, and why this was not previously recorded

The independent audit of 2026-08-19 found that P1.9C4 already executed a model fit
(`evaluate_model1.py:63`, `m2 = make_pipe(HGB()); m2.fit(dev, ydev)`) during Phase 0, and that no
document acknowledged the prohibition. `P1.9C4_MODEL1_INFERENCE_AMENDMENT.yaml:25` states
`retraining_recalibration_threshold_tuning: PROHIBITED`, while its sibling
`P1.9C4_MODEL1_EVALUATION_AMENDMENT.yaml:23` mandates fitting and reconciles the conflict only by
asserting that the fit "is the deterministic realisation of the frozen specification, not
retraining or tuning."

That reconciliation is substantively reasonable — the fit touches development data only and is
target-blind — but it was a stipulation made inside the document that needed it, with no appeal to
any prior rule and no reference to Phase 0. This waiver replaces that silence with an explicit
record, per audit-derived correction 7.

## 3. Exactly what is waived

Permitted under this waiver, and nothing else:

1. **Reproduction of the authoritative development results** (P1.10-B). Re-executing the frozen
   nested-CV procedure of `05_models.py` on the development dataset in order to verify that the
   published development metrics reproduce. Executed 2026-08-19; result EXACT
   (`docs/evidence/P1.10B_dev_m2_reproduction_receipt.json`, sha256 `a1476fd8…4624f`).
2. **Deterministic realisation of the frozen M2 specification** for deployment — fitting the frozen
   estimator on development data only, in order to score other cohorts.

Not waived, and still prohibited: training or tuning any *new* model; changing any hyperparameter,
feature, threshold rule or eligibility criterion; fitting on, or selecting anything with, any
evaluation cohort; Model 2 training of any kind.

## 4. Why this is scientifically safe

- The estimator, hyperparameters, featureset, seed, threshold rule, class orientation and feature
  order are the frozen ones, verified field-by-field against `05_models.py` and `08_p17_frozen.py`.
- No evaluation-cohort data participates in fitting or in threshold derivation.
- The reproduction is bit-exact against the pre-existing machine-readable development results, so
  the fit demonstrably reconstructs the historical model rather than a new one.

## 5. Standing conditions

- Every fit performed under this waiver must emit a machine-readable receipt recording input hash,
  environment, specification and outcome.
- The waiver expires when Phase 0 closes or when Model 1 v2 development begins, whichever is first.
  Model 1 v2 is **not** covered and requires its own authorisation.
