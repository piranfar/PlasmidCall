## 1. Verdict

> **The preregistered target was NOT MET, in both co-primary populations.**

The frozen target — PPV ≥ 0.95 **AND** recall > 0.50 at the preregistered standard operating point
**0.9605** — was applied to CP1 and CP2 separately, as the co-primary amendment requires. Precision
was met everywhere. Recall was met nowhere.

**This verdict is final for PlasmidCall v1.0.** It is not revised, softened or recomputed by the
later v1.1 deployment decision ([P1.10C](../plans/P1.10C_V1.1_DEPLOYMENT_OPERATING_POINT_AMENDMENT.yaml)).

### Why this verdict can be trusted

- The evaluation script was hashed **before predictions were joined to truth**:
  `443305dae12f09970cb21c7856aa23a62c28c899ff0b9f261b2e287f823bd171`, and was byte-identical at
  execution.
- The results record `threshold_derivation_performed_here: false`. The operating points were read in
  as constants; nothing about them was computed from P1.10.
- Truth was **absent from the execution host** for the whole of assembly and inference.
  `acquire_truth.py` refuses to run until `PREDICTIONS_FROZEN.json` exists.
- Identifier reconciliation was exact: 39/39 samples, 10,332 FASTA contigs = 10,332 matrix rows.

## 2. What the model actually is

PlasmidCall v1.0 is, on a strictly independent cohort, a **high-precision plasmid rule-in with recall
below target**. Seven false positives in 3,811 contigs: when it says plasmid, it is almost always
right. 324 of 626 true plasmid contigs are not selected: when it stays silent, that silence carries
little information.

`not_selected` is **not** a chromosome call, and it covers a large, systematically biased fraction of
true plasmid-borne contigs.

### Contig level and replicon level differ, and both are reported

| model | plasmid replicons | ≥1 TP | fully missed | replicon recall | contig recall |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|---|
| M0 | 130 | 100 | 30 | **0.769** | 0.404 |
| M2 @0.9605 | 130 | 88 | 42 | **0.677** | 0.482 |

Most plasmid *replicons* are detected by at least one contig even though under half of plasmid
*contigs* are selected. For deployment this distinction is material. Neither figure substitutes for
the other, and neither is quoted alone.

## 3. Threshold sensitivity — predeclared, and not a second test

The six development-derived operating points were reported as a predeclared sensitivity curve. They
are **descriptive**. The verdict is at 0.9605 and only at 0.9605.

| threshold | CP1 PPV | CP1 recall | CP2 PPV | CP2 recall |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.9378 | 0.9365 | 0.5619 | 0.9610 | 0.5911 |
| 0.9524 | 0.9636 | 0.5048 | 0.9707 | 0.5288 |
| **0.9605** ← preregistered | **0.9796** | **0.4571** | **0.9773** | **0.4824** |
| 0.9718 | 1.0000 | 0.3238 | 0.9872 | 0.3690 |
| 0.9732 | 1.0000 | 0.3238 | 0.9868 | 0.3594 |
| 0.9776 | 1.0000 | 0.2571 | 0.9889 | 0.2843 |

Recall exceeds 0.50 at 0.9378 and 0.9524. **That is not a pass.** Selecting a threshold after seeing
which one succeeds is precisely the threshold-realisation defect the independent audit identified in
P1.9, and the reason P1.10 was preregistered. The v1.1 deployment decision uses this curve openly and
labels the resulting figures as in-sample.

## 4. Uncertainty, sensitivity, composition

**Cluster bootstrap** — 10,000 replicates over the 39 independent genome clusters, seed 20260816.
Informational only; never a pass/fail gate.

```
CP2 M2 @0.9605    PPV    [0.947, 1.000]
CP2 M2 @0.9605    recall [0.396, 0.566]
CP1 M2 @0.9605    PPV    [0.940, 1.000]
CP1 M2 @0.9605    recall [0.259, 0.622]
```

The CP2 recall interval includes 0.50. The point estimate does not, and the interval is not used to
overturn the verdict.

**Leave-one-tool-out** at 0.9605, predeclared:

| panel | CP2 PPV | CP2 recall |
|---|---|---|
| full | 0.9773 | 0.4824 |
| − MOB-recon | 0.9684 | 0.4409 |
| − PLASMe | 0.9826 | 0.4505 |
| − geNomad | 0.9805 | 0.4808 |

Removing MOB-recon costs the most recall. On this cohort — which has **no** ANI ≥ 99.5 link to
MOB-suite's development corpus — that contribution is earned, not circular. It also shows the panel
is not robust to losing that member.

**Population composition.** CP1 holds 105 plasmid against 25 chromosome contigs. The co-primary
amendment anticipated a degenerate arm and required it be **reported, not swapped out**. Accordingly:
CP1's specificity (M0 0.160) and MCC have limited interpretability, and its PPV rests on 1–2 false
positives. The CP1 verdict stands, read with that caveat.

**Buckets, all 10,332 contigs**, preserved separately and never forced into a class:

```
below_1kb 6,376 · non_core_ARG_resolved 3,681 · resolved_core_ARG 130 · unmapped 86 · ambiguous 59
```

### Tool failures, recorded not repaired

PlasmidFinder crashed on 2 of 39 isolates — `IndexError` at `plasmidfinder.py:366`, an upstream
parser defect where a database header carries fewer than four `_`-separated fields. Deterministic,
data-dependent, and **not** a defect of this pipeline or of the assemblies.

The 691 affected contigs carry an explicit `FAILED` state, an 11-tool effective panel, and a flag.
The tool was **not patched**: doing so would have broken the image-identity gate, contradicted the
frozen rule that tool failures stay explicit, and constituted a post-hoc change to a tool after
observing its behaviour on the evaluation cohort. Sensitivity excluding flagged contigs is reported
(CP2 M2: n=3,593, PPV 0.9756, recall 0.4887) and does not change the verdict.

### Defects found and fixed during execution

Both are recorded on the block volume under `receipts/defects/`.

1. **Assembly copy-out permission** — the container runs as `mambauser(57439)`, host directories were
   `ubuntu(1001)` mode 755. Three assemblies completed then failed to write out. Re-run; no
   scientific effect. The frozen P1.9C4 runner already carried the fix; the P1.10 assembly runner had
   omitted it.
2. **Assembly concurrency race** — the driver gated on `docker ps`, which counts only containers that
   already exist. Under load `docker run` took ~40 s to create one, so nine launches raced through
   and ten containers ran on a 16-core host. No scientific effect: each container kept
   `--memory=12g --cpus=8` and `-t 8`, and SPAdes is deterministic for fixed threads. Replaced with
   an atomic slot that counts intent and fails closed. Measured afterwards: 10-way concurrency was
   **0.79×** the throughput of 2-way, confirming 2 already saturated the cores.

## 6. Independence, as measured

- **Sequence-level screen** against all 250 development isolates *and* the 8 P1.9 references — the
  first time in this programme that screen has been completed. 24 of 80 shortlisted candidates were
  excluded for ANI ≥ 99.5 links; 27 clonal pairs among the survivors were declustered to 39
  representatives.
- **Deposition-date floor 2025-09-11**, the day after the newest frozen panel database. No cohort
  genome can be inside any of the twelve tool databases.
- **Disclosed residual risks:** homology to pre-2016 relatives is generalisation, not leakage;
  MOB-suite's snapshot date is undocumented upstream; geographic skew toward DDBJ submissions;
  species scope is E. coli only against an Enterococcus-dominated development collection.

## 7. Status after this record

- **PlasmidCall v1.0** — independently validated; target **NOT MET** on recall. Verdict final.
- **PlasmidCall v1.1** — *validated beta*. Same frozen estimator, standard deployment threshold moved
  to **0.9524** with 0.9605 retained as the high-confidence tier. The operating point was selected
  with knowledge of these results and is **not independently validated**. See
  [P1.10C](../plans/P1.10C_V1.1_DEPLOYMENT_OPERATING_POINT_AMENDMENT.yaml) and
  [PLASMIDCALL_OUTPUT_CONTRACT_v1.1](../contracts/PLASMIDCALL_OUTPUT_CONTRACT_v1.1.yaml).
- **P1.10 is now a consumed cohort.** Like P1.9, it may never again serve as a fresh independent test,
  and no future threshold, calibration or model selection may use it.
