# The "~385 complete genomes" — catalogue reconciliation

**Date:** 2026-08-17 · **Stage:** P1.9B part B

---

## Decision

> # CATALOGUE_NOT_FOUND

No artifact in the Warehouse or in the three read-only related repositories contains 385
complete genomes, or 385 of any genome-related unit.

**More importantly: the figure has no located source at all.** "Approximately 385 complete
genomes" entered this project's documentation through the P1.9 instruction, and I recorded it
as given in `P1.9_controlled_benchmark_protocol_draft.yaml`, `P1.9_candidate_benchmarks.csv`
(C08) and `P1.9_decision_gate.md`. Those documents cite no evidence for it, because none was
supplied and none has now been found. It should be treated as an **unverified provisional
figure** until the owner identifies its origin.

Warehouse repository (full text and tables); `model1-context-mic`, `genotype-phenotype-mobility`,
`mobility-aware-amr-emergence` and `assay-aware-emergence-risk` (read-only): every
`*genome*`, `*assembl*`, `*gbff*`, `*census*` and `*accession*` table, plus a token search for
`385`. `AGENTS.md` routing and `LEGACY_SOURCE_INVENTORY.md` were used to target the search
rather than sweeping unrelated user directories.

Every `385` token hit was inside unrelated numeric data (AMRFinder databases, prediction
tables, Prokka output) — coincidental, not a catalogue count.

366, 360, 200, 137, 26 or 17 under any reading of assemblies, isolates, replicons or
post-filter counts, so `COUNT_RECONCILED_TO_DIFFERENT_UNIT` would overstate what was found.

## Consequence for the controlled benchmark

The controlled benchmark — Route 2 of `P1.9_decision_gate.md`, and now the critical path
since the external route is licence-blocked — was specified over an input pool of ~385
complete genomes. **That pool is not evidenced.**

The largest complete-genome pool actually located is **137** (`genome_metadata.tsv`, 7
species: *K. pneumoniae* 53, *S. aureus* 25, *P. aeruginosa* 24, *E. cloacae* 22,
*A. baumannii* 11, *E. hormaechei* 1, *E. asburiae* 1), plus **26** in the pilot crosswalk,
overlap between them not yet assessed.

This materially changes the design's feasibility and must be resolved before the protocol is
frozen:

- 137 genomes across 7 species, before genome-cluster deduplication, is a far smaller pool
  than 385. After clustering at 99.5% ANI the effective number of independent units will be
  smaller still.
- The mobile-chromosomal hard-negative layer needs closed chromosomes with mobile-element
  annotation; 137 is workable but leaves little room for a train/validation/test split at
  cluster level.
- *E. coli* is **absent** from the 137, which matters because *E. coli* is where the gplasCC
  reconstruction result was established.

**No replacement catalogue was created.** Per the instruction, a newly assembled pool would
have to be kept explicitly separate from the historical collection, and constructing one is
not authorised here.

2. Whether the controlled benchmark should proceed on the 137-genome pool, on a pool rebuilt

3. Confirmation of whether *E. coli* must be represented in the controlled benchmark; if so,
   the located pools do not currently supply it.

Until then the input-pool line in `P1.9_controlled_benchmark_protocol_draft.yaml` should be
read as unverified.
