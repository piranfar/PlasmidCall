# PlasmidCall v1.2-General — development evidence

**Status: `SUPPORTED BUT INCOMPLETE`.** Every number in this document is **retrospective**, derived
from data already consumed during development. None of it is independent confirmation. v1.2-General
is a **frozen development candidate** and must not be described as validated until the P1.11 primary
confirmatory analysis reports.

Frozen 2026-08-20. Authorized under [P1.12_PHASE0_FIT_WAIVER.md](../plans/P1.12_PHASE0_FIT_WAIVER.md).

## 1. Why a second model exists

PlasmidCall v1.1 was tuned for CP1, the core-ARG-bearing population. In the general population of
resolved contigs ≥ 1 kb it recovers about half of true plasmid contigs at its operating point:

| model | population | threshold | PPV | recall |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| v1.1 | P1.10 CP2 (n=3811, 626 plasmid) | 0.9524 | 0.9707 | 0.5288 |
| **v1.2-General** | P1.10 CP2 | **0.9285** | **0.9701** | **0.7764** |

TP 486 · FP 15 · TN 3170 · FN 140 · specificity 0.9953.

## 2. Feature selection — why categorical calls alone

A scoped 47-isolate probe compared, at **matched PPV**:

| candidate | recall at PPV ≥ 0.95 |
|---|---|
| v1.1 frozen | 0.7157 |
| **B — 12 categorical calls** | **0.9457** |
| C — 36 continuous native features | 0.8674 |
| categorical + Platon + MOB-recon continuous | 0.9457 |

Continuous native evidence, graph features and graph rescue added **nothing** at the deployment PPV
floor. Under the prespecified rule *prefer the simpler model when recall differs by less than 0.02*,
the **12 categorical tool calls are the minimal stable feature set**.

PlasmidHunter and gplasCC were not run — by instruction and by redundancy (plasmidCC's *E. coli*
model is adapted from PlaScope; plasmidCC replaces plasmidEC; gplasCC is a lightweight gplas2 — all
three already in the panel).

## 3. Threshold — why 0.9285

Selected by **target PPV 0.970**, not by maximising recall. Optimising *to* the 0.95 floor leaves a
margin of 0.0013 and **lowers** projected power, because with a complete panel the primary endpoint
is PPV-limited. Above 0.970 there is a cliff: at target 0.975 recall falls to 0.4329.

## 4. Matched-PPV frontier (P1.10 CP2)

## 7. Leakage and confound audit

**Permutation control.** Labels shuffled *within* isolate, 20 replicates, full leave-one-isolate-out
re-run each time: maximum recall across all 20 replicates **0.0000**, mean **0.0000**, against an
observed **0.7764**. The signal does not survive label destruction.

Group separation, in-fold preprocessing and P1.11 isolation are all verified programmatically.

## 8. Missing-tool neutrality — a defect found and corrected

**The defect.** A one-hot block followed by `StandardScaler` is *not* neutral when set to raw zero:
each column becomes `(0 − mean)/scale`, so an absent tool still pushed the logit. Measured on the
frozen model this reached **−2.97 logits** for PlasmidFinder (p 0.712 → 0.063), with an arbitrary
per-tool sign (PlaScope −1.63, plASgraph2 +1.03). A zero coefficient on the unseen `FAILED` column
did **not** make the block neutral — the neighbouring `chromosome` and `plasmid` columns carried the
shift.

**The correction.** A tool in a state never observed during training has its 8-column block imputed
to the training means, so the post-scaling block is exactly `0.0` and its logit contribution is
exactly `0.0`. States observed in training keep their learned encoding — `PlasmidFinder/FAILED`
(218 CP2 contigs) is the only one, and retains a fitted contribution of +0.353307.

**Verification**, measured through the actual scaler over all 24 tool-state combinations:

| quantity | value | required |
|---|---|---|
| max abs scaled value inside a neutralised block | 0.000e+00 | exactly 0 |
| max abs contribution of an absent unseen-state tool | 0.000e+00 | exactly 0 |
| every available tool bit-identical | true | true |
| regression guard: same figure *without* neutralisation | 1.0479 | must be > 1 |
| max abs residual from `n_valid` falling by one | 0.633400 | reported, not zero |

Neutralisation is a **strict no-op on the training data**, so the fitted estimator is unchanged and
clean complete-panel metrics are identical to before the correction.

**Scope.** The tool block is exactly neutral. The panel-summary block is deliberately *not* frozen:
an absent tool is excluded from `n_valid`, so those features honestly report a smaller panel.
Freezing them would mean pretending an absent tool had voted. That residual is measured above and
recorded in `P1.12_V1.2_NEUTRALITY_PROOF.json`.

**This is the single most important operational finding in the freeze.** The model degrades
*safely* — PPV holds or rises, so it does not make bad calls — but it degrades *sharply*: it simply
stops making calls. Losing PlaScope alone takes recall below the 0.50 primary floor. Losing four
tools yields no positive calls at all.

### Critical-tool gate

PlaScope is therefore a **prespecified critical component**. A preflight
([panel_preflight.py](../../scripts/p1_12/panel_preflight.py)) runs before any P1.11 inference and
verifies the frozen image, code, command, database hashes and parser version, then runs a
truth-blind smoke test on an **already consumed** sample requiring valid output, exact contig-ID
reconciliation and parser success. A critical failure exits non-zero and blocks processing.

During P1.11 a technical execution failure may be retried at most **twice** with the identical
frozen image, database, command and parameters. No tool may be patched, replaced or retuned once
processing begins. Affected samples are preserved, never discarded or relabelled, and coverage is
reported independently of truth.

Critical-tool availability is required for at least **95% of the 79 prespecified isolates (>= 76)**.
Below that the run is **OPERATIONALLY INCONCLUSIVE** - neither PASS nor FAIL. Verified end to end:

| critical coverage | gate | primary verdict |
|---|---|---|
| 79/79 = 100.0% | MET | scored normally |
| 76/79 = 96.2% | MET | scored, plus a separate failure-inclusive sensitivity analysis |
| 71/79 = 89.9% | NOT MET | **OPERATIONALLY INCONCLUSIVE**, reached even though PPV and recall both met their floors |

`PlasmidFinder/FAILED` remains permitted throughout - it was observed during training and carries a
defined learned encoding. All other tools preserve explicit `FAILED`/`MISSING` states.

## 12. Interpreting the coefficients

The fitted weights are **conditional multivariable coefficients**. Each is the contribution of one
tool state *given every other tool in the panel*. They must **not** be read as standalone biological
tool effects, as measures of a tool's accuracy, or as a ranking of tool quality.

§9 and §10 make the point concretely: PlasmidFinder carries a large positive coefficient
(+0.5187 for `plasmid`) yet removing it and refitting *improves* recall by 0.0160 — its information
is largely carried by correlated tools. Conversely PlaScope has a modest coefficient but its loss at
inference is catastrophic. Coefficient magnitude, refit ablation and deployment failure impact are
three different quantities and disagree substantially.

## 13. Stated limitations

1. **Not independently validated.** All figures are retrospective on consumed data.
2. **Fitted on 39 isolates.** P1.9 excluded from the fit, costing 8 isolates of training signal.
3. **Three isolates regressed** relative to v1.1 (§5).
4. **64 of 102 features are inert** — zero variance in training, exactly-zero coefficient. The
   effective model is 38 features.
5. **23 of 24 failure/missing states were never observed**, so they are neutral by construction
   rather than by evidence. The model cannot separate states it never saw.
6. **v1.1 is better above PPV 0.98** (§4). The advantage is operating-point-specific.
7. **Severe single-tool failure sensitivity** (§10). Panel completeness is an operational
   precondition.
8. **CP1 is structurally unpowerable** at any reachable N; the v1.1 secondary analysis is descriptive.
9. **PlasmidFinder failed upstream** on 2 isolates (`IndexError` at `plasmidfinder.py:366`). Recorded
   as explicit `FAILED` and deliberately **not patched** — patching would break image identity and
   would be a post-hoc change made after observing behaviour on the evaluation cohort.
