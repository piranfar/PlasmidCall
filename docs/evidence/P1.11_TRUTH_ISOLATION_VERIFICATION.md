## Why item 7 holds — the container boundary

Truth-blindness is enforced structurally, not by convention:

- The only paths any inference container can open are the **five** mount specs above
  (`$IN:/in:ro`, `$DB:/db:ro`, `$OUT:/out`, `$DB/plasme/DB:/dbw`, and the PlasmidEC→gplas2
  `/pec:ro` handoff). A reference FASTA at `p111/screen/genomes/` is outside every one of them.
- The sealed cohort record is likewise unmounted — `run_tool.sh` has no reference to
  `manifests/` or to either cohort file.
- The panel tools receive **only** the Product A assembly for the sample they are scoring, plus their
  own read-only database and their own output directory.

## Why a reference FASTA is not truth

**A correction to an earlier draft of this page.** It claimed there is "no object anywhere in the
P1.11 tree that says *this contig is plasmid*". That is **false**, and the false version overstated
the guarantee. The reference FASTA deflines *do* label replicons — measured, **79 of 79** cohort
FASTAs contain the word `plasmid` in at least one defline:

```
>AP040898.1 Escherichia coli c1-17 DNA, complete genome
>AP040899.1 Escherichia coli c1-17 plasmid pCTX-M-2_c1-17 DNA, complete genome
>AP040900.1 Escherichia coli c1-17 plasmid pc1-17_A DNA, complete genome
```

So replicon-level plasmid annotation of the *reference* is on the host. The accurate claim is
narrower and turns on the distinction the whole truth protocol rests on:

> A defline labels a **replicon of the reference genome**. Truth is a label on a **contig of the
> short-read assembly**. Those are different objects, and nothing on the host connects them.

A truth call requires three things. Only the first exists:

| # | requirement | state |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | the reference sequence, with its replicon deflines | **present** (ANI screen) |
| 2 | the minimap2 contig→replicon mapping at identity 0.95 / coverage 0.80 / margin 0.10 / min length 1000 | **absent** — 0 `*.paf` under `p112` |
| 3 | authoritative replicon roles from `_assembly_report.txt` (`Assigned-Molecule-Location/Type`) | **absent** — 0 under `p112` |

Item 3 matters even though the deflines exist: the frozen protocol takes replicon roles from the
NCBI assembly report, **not** from defline text, precisely because free-text deflines are not an
authoritative role source.

Without (2), no contig is associated with any replicon. **No object on the host assigns a role to
any contig of any P1.11 assembly** — which is the claim that actually matters, and the one the
evidence supports.

## Provenance of the reference FASTAs

They were downloaded by `p111/tools/fetch_candidates.py` for the **ANI independence screen** — the
step that proved the P1.11 cohort non-overlapping with the development set, P1.9 and P1.10 at
ANI ≥ 0.995. Their presence is a property of cohort construction and **predates** panel inference.
It is not a leak into inference, and it is not evidence of a truth operation.

**0 of the 79 P1.11 cohort isolates has any truth artefact anywhere on the volume.**

## Panel state at verification

Undisturbed throughout. At the time of writing: 595 of 1,027 runs complete, 4 `run_tool.sh`
processes executing, single-instance lock held, PlaScope 79/79. The panel advanced normally during
and after these checks (615 done at the last reading) — a rising count is the healthy direction.

**On the failure count.** The **6** figure is the number of `.failed` *state markers*, which record
the FINAL outcome per tool/sample after the prespecified retry rule. Separately, **53** receipts
under `p112/inference/receipts/` carry `"status": "FAILED"` — those include superseded retry
attempts (each failure is retried up to twice with an identical image, database, command and
parameters). The two numbers measure different things and neither is wrong; 6 is the count of
tool/sample pairs that ultimately failed, and it is the figure the completeness gate uses.

No panel process was stopped, restarted or reconfigured to perform any check on this page; every
measurement is read-only.
