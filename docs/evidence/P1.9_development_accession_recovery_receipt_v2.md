## Disclosure obligation this creates

That the development collection is the isolate set of a published benchmark is a **provenance fact
that must be disclosed** in any manuscript, together with the citation. It is not a defect: the
isolates are public, the P1.11 validation cohort is disjoint from them by identifier and by
sequence, and no P1.11 truth or metadata entered model development. But a reader who discovered the
relationship independently would reasonably question anything that had not stated it.

Two consequences follow for interpretation, and both are recorded in
`docs/evidence/P1.11_MANUSCRIPT_POSITIONING_RECEIPT.md`:

1. **PlasmidCall v1.1 was developed on the Teixeira benchmark collection.** Its evaluation on
   E. coli is a species-shifted generalisation test, and the development set must be cited as
   theirs.
2. **The tool-ranking agreement is only partly independent.** The *evaluation* cohorts differ —
   their ranking comes from these 250 isolates, ours from the disjoint 79-isolate P1.11 cohort — so
   the agreement of the leading four tools is a genuine cross-cohort reproduction. But the panel of
   tools is the same, and v1.1's development data is their benchmark set, so the reproduction is
   not independent in every respect and must not be described as though it were.
